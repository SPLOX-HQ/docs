---
title: "Extra usage"
description: "The wallet: what it pays for, when it is used instead of the plan, and how spend is shown"
---

Extra usage is a wallet you put dollars into. It pays for work once a plan
window is full, at [list prices](/account/model-prices), so work does not stop at the cap on a plan you
are otherwise happy with. It is on Pro and Max; a free account cannot fill it.

The wallet sits under **Extra usage** on **Usage & billing**, and it shows three
things:

| On screen | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| The balance | What is in the wallet, in dollars. |
| *"Extra usage is on."* / *"Extra usage is off."* | Whether the wallet holds anything. There is no switch to flip: extra usage is on whenever there is money in it. |
| **$0.00 spent** | What the wallet has paid for in the current billing period — your subscription's own period, from one renewal to the next, not a calendar month. |

## When the wallet pays

Who pays is decided once for each piece of work, before it runs, and it is
final. One piece of work is never split between the plan and the wallet.

| Situation | Who pays |
| --- | --- |
| Neither window is full | the plan |
| A window is full, the wallet covers the work | the wallet, at [list price](/account/model-prices) |
| A window is full, the wallet cannot | nobody — [the request is refused](/account/limits) |

The plan and the wallet are two different price scales, and the wallet's is the
list one: the same work costs more out of the wallet than out of the plan
allowance. That is the trade — the plan is the discount you bought, extra usage
is what you pay when you have spent it faster than the pace you bought.

The wallet cannot go negative. Extra usage is only granted when the balance
already covers the work, and if a final charge somehow does not fit, the wallet
is taken to zero rather than below it — so the next request is refused instead
of let through against money that can never pay.

## Topping up

<Frame caption="The top-up amounts, and the tax line before checkout">
  <img src="/images/account/top-up.png" alt="The top-up dialog with $10 through $500 presets" />
</Frame>

**Top up** on the **Extra usage** section opens this. Six amounts, and only
those six: $10, $25, $50, $100, $250 and $500. Pick one and you are sent to
Stripe to pay. Only one checkout is open at a time: starting a new one closes
the last.

<Note>
The dialog previews tax at a flat 20%. What you are actually charged is set by
Stripe Tax from the billing address on the account, so the total at checkout can
differ from the total in the preview. The wallet is credited with the amount you
picked, before tax.
</Note>

On a free account there is nothing to top up: **Top up** tells you *"Top-ups are
available on Pro and Max plans. Upgrade to enable."*

## Running low

Two different things can be wrong with a wallet, and the product treats them
differently.

**Low, not empty.** Below $0.50 with the plan windows full, a banner appears
across the top: *"Your balance is low ($0.31). Top up to keep running
harnesses."* It can be dismissed, and it comes back the next time the balance
falls under the line. Free accounts never see it — there is no wallet behind it
to act on.

**Nothing left.** Work that needs the wallet and finds it under $0.50 stops with
*"Your balance is below $0.50. Top up to continue."*

In the app this one *does* open the top-up dialog, because money is genuinely
what fixes it. That is the whole reason it is told apart from a full window —
see [Limits](/account/limits).

## Where the spend is shown

**Usage & billing** shows two figures: the wallet balance, which leads because
it is the number you act on, and what was spent this cycle underneath it.

Every individual charge and top-up is on **Transactions**:

<Frame caption="Transactions: every charge and top-up on the account">
  <img src="/images/account/transactions.png" alt="The Transactions screen with no transactions yet" />
</Frame>

By default the list covers the current calendar month, so a charge from an
earlier month is outside the range rather than missing.

This list is wallet movement, not invoices. Invoices and receipts for the
subscription itself live in the Stripe portal; [Billing](/account/billing) says
how to get there.
